SCREEN ART (Odd-Ratio Screen Rule, chosen by js/stage.js)
- Closest art by shape. Cut-off = (ratio A + ratio B) / 2 for neighbors.
- Squarer art below the cut-off, wider art above it.
- Letterbox leftover space. Never stretch or crop.
- No special cases for any one device.
- Art ratios live in ONE place: the ARTS list in js/stage.js.
